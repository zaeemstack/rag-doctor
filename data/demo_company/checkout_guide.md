# Nova Retail — Checkout Guide

**Last updated:** 1 April 2025

A step-by-step walkthrough of the Nova Retail checkout experience.

---

## Step 1: Review Your Cart

Before proceeding to checkout, review items in your cart at **novaretail.com/cart**:

- Confirm quantities and sizes/variants
- Apply any promotional or discount codes in the **"Have a promo code?"** field
- Review the estimated order total (tax and shipping are calculated in the next step)

---

## Step 2: Enter or Confirm Delivery Address

If you are logged in, your saved addresses will appear. You may:

- Select an existing address
- Add a new shipping address
- Ship to multiple addresses (available for orders with 3+ items — select "Ship to Multiple Addresses" at the top of the address form)

**PO Boxes:** Accepted for USPS Standard shipping only.

---

## Step 3: Select a Shipping Method

After entering your address, available shipping options and their costs are displayed:

| Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | **5–7 business days** from dispatch | $5.99 (free ≥ $75) |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 |
| Same-Day | Today (metro areas only) | $9.99 |

**Tip:** Standard shipping is automatically upgraded to free when your subtotal reaches $75 before tax.

---

## Step 4: Choose a Payment Method

Nova Retail accepts the following payment methods:

- **Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover** (credit & debit)
- **PayPal** — redirects to PayPal to complete payment
- **Nova Gift Card** — enter the 16-digit card code; multiple gift cards accepted per order
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** — available in compatible browsers
- **Klarna** (Buy Now, Pay Later) — pay in 4 interest-free instalments
- **Afterpay** — pay in 4 instalments over 6 weeks

Nova Rewards points can be applied alongside any payment method to reduce the order total.

---

## Step 5: Review and Place Order

The order summary page shows:

- Itemised product list with prices
- Applied discount codes and rewards points
- Shipping cost and method
- Estimated tax (calculated based on delivery address)
- **Order total**

Review carefully before clicking **"Place Order."** By placing an order you agree to Nova Retail's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

---

## Step 6: Order Confirmation

After placing your order:

1. You will see an on-screen **order confirmation** with your order number.
2. A confirmation email is sent to your registered address within **5 minutes**.
3. A **dispatch notification** with tracking number is sent when your order ships.

---

## Order Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Payment is being verified |
| Processing | Order is being picked and packed |
| Dispatched | Handed to the carrier; tracking is active |
| Out for Delivery | With local carrier for final delivery |
| Delivered | Carrier confirmed delivery |
| Cancelled | Order was cancelled before dispatch |

## Common Checkout Issues

### My promo code isn't working
- Check the expiry date and minimum spend requirement
- Codes are case-sensitive — copy-paste to avoid typos
- Some codes are single-use or tied to a specific account
- Codes cannot be combined unless explicitly stated

### My payment was declined
- Verify your billing address matches your bank's records
- Try an alternative payment method
- Contact your bank if the issue persists

### I didn't receive a confirmation email
- Check your spam / junk folder
- Add **noreply@novaretail.com** to your safe senders list
- Log in to **My Account → Orders** — your order will appear there even without the email
