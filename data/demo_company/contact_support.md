## Reporting Fraud or Security Issues

If you suspect fraudulent activity on your account or have found a security vulnerability in our systems:

- **Account fraud:** Contact support immediately at 1-800-NOVA-RET. We will place a security hold on your account.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** Email **security@novaretail.com** with details. We operate a responsible disclosure programme.
