# Nova Retail — Order Tracking

**Last updated:** 1 February 2025

---

## How to Track Your Order

### Option 1: Track via novaretail.com
1. Visit **novaretail.com/track**.
2. Enter your **order number** (format: NR-XXXXXXXX) and the **email address** used at checkout.
3. Your order status, current location, and estimated delivery date are displayed.

### Option 2: Track via My Account
If you are logged in, go to **My Account → Orders** and click **"Track"** next to any dispatched order.

### Option 3: Track Directly with the Carrier
Once your order ships, you receive a tracking number in your dispatch confirmation email. Use this number directly on:

| Carrier | Tracking URL |
|---|---|
| UPS | ups.com/track |
| FedEx | fedex.com/tracking |
| USPS | tools.usps.com/go/TrackConfirmAction |
| DHL | dhl.com/en/express/tracking |

## Order Status Guide

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your payment is being verified. No action needed. |
| **Processing** | Your order is being picked and packed at our fulfilment centre. |
| **Dispatched** | Your order has been handed to the carrier. Tracking is now active. |
| **In Transit** | Your order is moving through the carrier's network. |
| **Out for Delivery** | Your order is with the local carrier and will be delivered today. |
| **Delivery Attempted** | A delivery attempt was made; a notice was left or a re-delivery is scheduled. |
| **Delivered** | Carrier has confirmed delivery. |
| **Return in Progress** | A return has been initiated and the item is on its way back to us. |
| **Refund Processed** | Your return was received and refund has been issued. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled before dispatch. |

## Tracking Not Updating?

Tracking information can take up to **24 hours** to appear after dispatch. Common reasons for no updates:

- The carrier has not yet scanned the parcel at their facility.
- Weekend or public holiday — scans may be delayed.
- International orders entering customs — tracking pauses during customs clearance.

If there is **no movement for 3+ business days** (domestic) or **5+ business days** (international), contact **shipping@novaretail.com** with your order number.

## Missed Deliveries

If you missed a delivery:

1. Check the carrier notice left at your door for re-delivery or pickup instructions.
2. Use the carrier's website to schedule a re-delivery or redirect to a pickup location.
3. Most carriers hold parcels at a local facility for **5 business days** before returning to sender.

If the parcel is returned to Nova Retail, we will email you. You can choose to have it re-shipped (standard shipping fee applies) or receive a full refund.

## Lost Packages

If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you have not received the package:

1. Check with neighbours, the building lobby, or any safe location the carrier might have left the parcel.
2. Wait 24 hours — "Delivered" scans can occasionally fire slightly early.
3. If still missing, contact us at **support@novaretail.com** within **30 days** of the expected delivery date. We will open a carrier investigation.

Investigation timelines:
- UPS / FedEx: 5–7 business days
- USPS: 7–10 business days
- DHL (international): 10–15 business days

If the investigation confirms loss, we will reship the order or issue a full refund.
