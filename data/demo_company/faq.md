# Nova Retail — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Last updated:** 15 February 2025

---

## Orders & Checkout

**Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?**  
A: Orders can be modified within **1 hour** of placement. Contact live chat immediately. Once an order enters the "Processing" status, modifications are no longer possible.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**  
A: Yes, cancellations are accepted within **2 hours** of order placement, provided the order has not yet been dispatched. Use the "Cancel Order" button in **My Account → Orders**, or contact support.

**Q: Why was my payment declined?**  
A: Common reasons include: insufficient funds, incorrect card details, billing address mismatch, or a bank security hold. Try a different payment method or contact your bank. Nova Retail does not store or have visibility into your bank's decision.

**Q: Is it safe to use my credit card on novaretail.com?**  
A: Yes. All transactions are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors. Nova Retail does not store raw card numbers.

**Q: Do you ship to PO Boxes?**  
A: Yes, via USPS for standard shipping. Expedited and overnight services cannot be delivered to PO Boxes.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: What is Nova Retail's return policy?**  
A: Our return window is **30 days** from the date of delivery (aligned with Refund Policy). Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

**Q: How do I start a return?**  
A: Log in to **novaretail.com/account**, go to **Orders → Request Return**, select your item and reason, then download the prepaid return label.

**Q: When will I receive my refund?**  
A: After we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days for card payments, or 1–3 business days for PayPal.

**Q: Can I return an item bought as a gift?**  
A: Yes, with the gift receipt or order number. The refund will be issued as a Nova Gift Card credit.

**Q: Are Final Sale items returnable?**  
A: No. Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout are not eligible for return or exchange under any circumstances.

## Products & Warranty

**Q: What is covered under the Nova Retail warranty?**  
A: Nova-branded products come with a limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. See the full Warranty Policy for details on duration, coverage, and the claim process.

**Q: Can I buy an extended warranty?**  
A: Yes. The Nova Protection Plan can be added at checkout or within 30 days of purchase. Plans range from +1 to +3 additional years of coverage.

**Q: Do you sell third-party products?**  
A: Yes, through our marketplace. Third-party products are covered by their manufacturer's warranty, not Nova Retail's.

## Account & Loyalty

**Q: How do I join the Nova Rewards programme?**  
A: Create a free account at **novaretail.com/signup**. Rewards points begin accruing on your very first purchase.

**Q: How do I redeem Nova Rewards points?**  
A: At checkout, select "Apply Rewards Points." 100 points = $1.00 in discount. Minimum redemption is 500 points ($5.00).

**Q: Can I use a gift card online?**  
A: Yes. Enter the gift card code in the "Payment" section at checkout. Multiple gift cards can be stacked on a single order.

## Privacy & Security

**Q: How does Nova Retail use my personal data?**  
A: We collect data to process orders, improve services, and send relevant communications (with your consent). We do not sell personal data to third parties. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Email **privacy@novaretail.com** with "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days in accordance with applicable privacy law.
