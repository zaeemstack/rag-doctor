# Nova Retail — Warranty Policy

**Effective date:** 1 March 2025  
**Document owner:** Product Quality Team  
**Version:** 2.5

---

## 1. Limited Warranty Coverage

Nova Retail provides a **1-year** limited warranty on all Nova-branded products, covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use and service conditions. This warranty is valid from the **original purchase date** as shown on the customer's receipt.

---

## 2. What Is Covered

The warranty covers:

- Manufacturing defects (hardware failures not caused by user damage)
- Faulty components present at the time of purchase
- Premature failure of mechanical or electronic parts under normal use

---

## 3. What Is NOT Covered

The warranty **does not** cover:

- Accidental damage (drops, spills, physical impact)
- Damage caused by unauthorised modifications or repair
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, battery capacity loss after cycles)
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Damage caused by operating the product outside its intended environment (e.g. extreme temperatures, humidity)
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers
- Third-party products sold through Nova Retail's marketplace — those are covered by the respective manufacturer's warranty

---

## 4. Warranty Claim Process

1. Gather your **proof of purchase** (order confirmation email or receipt) and the product's **serial number**.
2. Visit **novaretail.com/warranty** or call **1-800-NOVA-WTY** (1-800-668-2989).
3. Complete the online warranty claim form — you will receive a claim reference number within 1 business day.
4. Ship the product to our authorised service centre (prepaid label provided for domestic claims).
5. Our technicians will inspect the product within **7 business days** of receipt.
6. Outcome options: repair, replacement with an equivalent unit, or store credit.

---

## 5. Warranty Transferability

The Nova Retail limited warranty applies to the **original purchaser only** and is non-transferable. Warranty rights are not extended to subsequent owners.

---

## 6. Extended Warranty Plans

Customers may purchase a **Nova Protection Plan** at the time of checkout or within **30 days** of purchase, extending coverage for up to **3 additional years** beyond the standard warranty period. The Nova Protection Plan also covers accidental damage (1 incident per plan year, subject to a service fee of $29.99).

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Nova Retail's liability under this warranty is limited to the original purchase price of the product. Nova Retail is not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
