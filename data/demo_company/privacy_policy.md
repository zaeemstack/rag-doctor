# Nova Retail — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** Legal & Compliance Team  
**Version:** 5.0

---

## 1. Introduction

Nova Retail, Inc. ("Nova Retail," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

This policy applies to information collected via novaretail.com, the Nova Retail mobile app, and in our physical stores.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account information:** name, email address, password (hashed), phone number
- **Order information:** billing and shipping address, payment method details (card last 4 digits only — we do not store full card numbers)
- **Communications:** emails, live chat transcripts, and phone call recordings (where permitted by law)
- **Reviews and feedback:** product reviews and survey responses

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage data:** pages visited, search queries, items viewed, time on site
- **Device data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
- **Location data:** general location inferred from IP address; precise location (with your permission) via the mobile app for same-day delivery eligibility

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Payment processors:** transaction confirmation and fraud signals
- **Social login providers (Google, Apple):** profile name and email address if you choose to log in via these services
- **Analytics providers:** aggregated, de-identified browsing data

## 4. Data Sharing

Nova Retail does **not sell** your personal information to third parties.

We share data with:
- **Service providers:** payment processors, shipping carriers, cloud hosting, analytics (bound by data processing agreements)
- **Legal authorities:** when required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of persons
- **Business transfers:** in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (with notice to you)

## 6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

- **Essential cookies:** login sessions, shopping cart (cannot be disabled)
- **Analytics cookies:** site usage statistics (opt-out available)
- **Marketing cookies:** cross-site advertising (opt-out available via cookie banner)

Manage your cookie preferences at any time via **novaretail.com/cookie-settings**.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Restrict** or **object to** certain processing
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (does not affect prior processing)

**California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** You have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell data, but you may opt out of cross-context behavioural advertising).

**EU/UK residents (GDPR):** You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email **privacy@novaretail.com** or complete the form at **novaretail.com/privacy-request**. We will respond within **30 days** (or sooner as required by law).

---

## 8. Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive data, access controls, and regular penetration testing. No system is 100 % secure; in the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you as required by law.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

Nova Retail's services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact **privacy@novaretail.com** for deletion.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We will post changes to this policy with a new "Effective date" and notify registered users by email for material changes at least **30 days** before the change takes effect.
