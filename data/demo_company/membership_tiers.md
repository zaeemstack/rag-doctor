# Nova Retail — Membership Tiers

**Last updated:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** Customer Loyalty Team

---

## Overview

Nova Retail's membership tier system rewards loyal customers with increasing benefits as their annual spend grows. Tiers are based on **calendar-year** spending (1 January – 31 December) and are assessed monthly.

---

## Tier Structure

| Tier | Annual Spend Threshold | Colour Badge |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | $0 – $499 | 🟤 Bronze |
| Silver | $500 – $1,499 | ⚪ Silver |
| Gold | $1,500 – $3,999 | 🟡 Gold |
| Elite | $4,000+ | 💜 Elite Purple |

---

## Benefits by Tier

### Bronze (Entry Level)
- Earn **1–3 points per $1** spent (1 pt on third-party, 3 pts on Nova-branded)
- Access to member-only sale previews (24 hours before public)
- Birthday bonus: **500 Rewards points**

### Silver
Everything in Bronze, plus:
- **5 % discount** on all Nova-branded products every day
- Priority live chat queue (estimated wait under 1 minute)
- Dedicated email support (response within 4 hours on business days)
- Access to the Silver member-only newsletter with early restocking alerts

### Gold
Everything in Silver, plus:
- **10 % discount** on all items (Nova-branded and third-party)
- **Free Expedited Shipping** on every order (waives the $12.99 fee)
- Exclusive access to Beta Products — test new products before public launch
- Invitation to bi-annual member webinars with the Nova product team

### Elite
Everything in Gold, plus:
- **15 % discount** on all items
- **72-hour early access** to all sale events (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Holiday Sale, etc.)
- Dedicated account manager (personal point of contact for B2C queries)
- Annual gift: a curated selection of new Nova-branded products (shipped free)
- Complimentary Nova Protection Plan (Basic tier) on one purchase per year
- Invitation to the annual Nova Elite Summit (in-person event, Columbus OH)

---

## Tier Progression Rules

1. **Calculation:** Spend is based on the amount paid after discounts and Rewards points, but **before** tax and shipping.
2. **Real-time updates:** The system checks spend totals at the end of each calendar month. If you hit the threshold mid-month, your tier updates at month-end.
3. **Immediate benefits:** Discounts and perks begin the day your tier changes.
4. **Annual reset:** All tiers reset to Bronze on **1 January** of each year, regardless of prior status.
5. **Re-qualifying:** You must re-reach the spend threshold each year to retain or regain a higher tier.

---

## Losing a Tier

If you are on a higher tier and do not re-qualify by 31 December, you revert to the tier matching your calendar-year spend on 1 January. Your Rewards points balance is **not affected** by a tier change.

## Combining Tier Discounts with Other Offers

| Combination | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Tier discount + Rewards points redemption | ✓ Yes |
| Tier discount + gift card | ✓ Yes |
| Tier discount + promotional / coupon code | ✗ No — the greater of the two is applied |
| Tier discount + employee discount | ✗ No — employee discount takes precedence |

---

## Tier Verification for In-Store Purchases

To receive your tier discount in a physical store:

1. Open the Nova Retail app and show your **digital membership card**, or
2. Provide your registered email address to the sales associate.

In-store purchases count towards your annual spend total.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I earn tier status through gift card purchases?**  
A: No. Gift card purchases do not count towards annual spend for tier calculation purposes.

**Q: Does business (B2B) spend count towards my personal tier?**  
A: No. Business account spend is tracked separately and does not contribute to personal tier status.

**Q: What happens to my Rewards points if I downgrade tiers?**  
A: Points are unaffected by tier changes. Your balance remains intact.
