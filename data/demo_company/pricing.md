# Nova Retail — Pricing & Discounts

**Last updated:** 1 March 2025  
**Document owner:** Finance & Merchandising

---

## 1. Pricing Principles

Nova Retail prices all products at **Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP)** for third-party brands. Nova-branded products are priced to deliver at least **20 % savings** compared to comparable third-party products.

Prices are displayed in USD and are inclusive of any applicable product-level fees. Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address and applicable state/local tax rates.

---

## 2. Member Discounts

| Membership Tier | Everyday Discount | Stacking Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 0 % (standard price) | N/A |
| Silver | 5 % on Nova-branded items | Cannot stack with promo codes |
| Gold | 10 % on all items site-wide | Cannot stack with promo codes |
| Elite | 15 % on all items + early sale access | Cannot stack with promo codes |

See the Membership Tiers document for how to achieve each tier.

---

## 3. Volume Pricing (Business Customers)

Business accounts placing orders of **10+ units** of the same SKU qualify for volume pricing:

| Units per SKU | Discount |
|---|---|
| 10–24 | 8 % |
| 25–49 | 12 % |
| 50–99 | 18 % |
| 100+ | 22 % (custom quote required) |

Contact **b2b@novaretail.com** to register a business account and unlock volume pricing.

## 4. Sale Events

Nova Retail runs the following planned sale events each year:

| Event | Typical Discount | Period |
|---|---|---|
| New Year Sale | 10–30 % | 1–7 January |
| Spring Refresh | 15–25 % | Late March / Early April |
| Mid-Year Clearance | 20–40 % | Late June |
| Back to School | 10–20 % | August |
| Black Friday | 25–50 % | Last week of November |
| Cyber Monday | 20–45 % | First Monday of December |
| Holiday Sale | 15–35 % | 10–24 December |

Exact dates and discounts are announced via email and on novaretail.com 7 days before each event.

---

## 5. Promotional Codes

- Promo codes are distributed via email newsletter, social media, and partner promotions.
- Each code specifies its minimum cart value, eligible categories, and expiry date.
- Codes are single-use per customer unless stated otherwise.
- Codes cannot be combined with each other.
- Member discounts (Silver/Gold/Elite) cannot be applied alongside promo codes. The system applies the greater discount automatically.

---

## 6. Dynamic Pricing

Nova Retail does not engage in surge pricing. Prices for a given SKU are the same for all customers at any given moment. Prices may change over time in response to supplier costs, market conditions, or promotional schedules.

---

## 7. Currency & International Pricing

Prices on novaretail.com are displayed in USD. International customers pay in USD; currency conversion is handled by the customer's bank or payment provider. Nova Retail does not control exchange rates or conversion fees.

---

## 8. Price Corrections

If a product is listed at an incorrect price due to a typographical error or system fault, Nova Retail reserves the right to cancel orders placed at the incorrect price. Affected customers will be notified and offered the option to reorder at the correct price or receive a full refund.

---

*Prices and discount percentages are subject to change without notice.*
