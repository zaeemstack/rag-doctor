# Nova Retail — Shipping Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** Logistics & Fulfilment Team  
**Version:** 2.8

---

## 1. Overview

Nova Retail ships to all 50 US states, US territories, and 40+ international destinations. All orders are processed from our fulfilment centres in Columbus (OH) and Reno (NV).

---

## 2. Order Processing

- Orders placed before **14:00 local fulfilment-centre time** on a business day are processed the same day.
- Orders placed after 14:00 or on weekends/public holidays are processed the next business day.
- You will receive a shipment confirmation email with a tracking number within 2 hours of dispatch.

---

## 3. Domestic Shipping Options

| Service | Estimated Transit | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | **3–5 business days** | $5.99 (free on orders ≥ $75) |
| Expedited Shipping | 2 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight Shipping | Next business day (order by 12:00 ET) | $24.99 |
| Same-Day Delivery | Available in select metro areas | $9.99 |

Free standard shipping is automatically applied at checkout for qualifying orders; no coupon code required.

## 5. International Shipping

Nova Retail ships internationally to 40+ countries. International shipping rates and estimated delivery times vary by destination.

| Region | Estimated Transit | Starting Price |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | 5–7 business days | $14.99 |
| European Union | 7–12 business days | $19.99 |
| United Kingdom | 7–10 business days | $17.99 |
| Australia / NZ | 10–14 business days | $22.99 |
| Rest of World | 12–21 business days | $24.99 |

**Important:** International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, and local fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in Nova Retail's shipping price.

---

## 6. Shipment Tracking

- Domestic orders: Track via **novaretail.com/track** or directly on the carrier website using the tracking number in your confirmation email.
- International orders: Tracking is provided until the parcel enters the destination country's postal network; local tracking may be limited thereafter.

---

## 7. Lost or Stolen Packages

If your tracking shows "Delivered" but the package is missing:

1. Check with neighbours and building management.
2. Wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark packages delivered slightly early.
3. Contact support at **support@novaretail.com** with your order number. We will open a carrier investigation within 1 business day.

Claims for lost packages must be submitted within **30 days** of the expected delivery date.

---

## 8. Domestic Returns Shipping

For approved domestic returns, Nova Retail provides a **prepaid return shipping label** at no cost to the customer. See the Refund & Return Policy for full details on initiating a return.

> **Note:** This section covers domestic (US) return shipping only.

---

## 9. Shipping Carriers

Nova Retail partners with:

- UPS (primary domestic carrier)
- FedEx (expedited and overnight)
- USPS (standard and PO Box deliveries)
- DHL (primary international carrier)

Carrier selection is made automatically based on your location and selected service level.

---

## 10. Address Changes

Once an order is dispatched, address changes are not guaranteed. Contact support immediately at **1-800-NOVA-RET** and we will attempt to redirect the shipment with the carrier — subject to carrier availability.
