# Nova Retail — Refund & Return Policy

**Effective date:** 1 March 2025  
**Document owner:** Customer Experience Team  
**Version:** 3.2

---

## 1. Overview

Nova Retail is committed to customer satisfaction. If you are not completely happy with your purchase, we offer a straightforward return and refund process subject to the conditions below.

---

## 2. Return Window

Customers may return eligible items within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. Returns initiated after 30 days will not be accepted unless the item is covered under a warranty claim (see the Warranty Policy document).

---

## 3. Eligible Items

The following product categories are eligible for return:

- Consumer electronics (unopened or opened with original packaging)
- Clothing and footwear (unworn, with original tags attached)
- Home goods and kitchenware
- Nova-branded accessories

### 3.1 Non-Eligible Items

The following items **cannot** be returned:

- Perishable goods (food, flowers, plants)
- Digital downloads and software licence keys once activated
- Personalised or custom-engraved items
- Hazardous materials
- Items marked **Final Sale** at the time of purchase
- Hygiene products (earphones, underwear) once opened

---

## 4. Condition Requirements

Returned items must be:

1. In their original condition — unused, undamaged, and with all accessories included.
2. In original packaging where applicable.
3. Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

Nova Retail reserves the right to refuse returns that do not meet these requirements or to apply a restocking fee of up to **15 %** of the item price.

---

## 5. How to Start a Return

1. Log in to your Nova Retail account at **novaretail.com/account**.
2. Navigate to **Orders → Request Return**.
3. Select the item(s) and reason for return.
4. Print or save the prepaid return shipping label (domestic orders only).
5. Drop the parcel at any authorised carrier location within **7 calendar days** of label generation.

---

## 6. Refund Processing

| Payment method | Refund timeline after item received |
|---|---|
| Credit / debit card | 3–5 business days |
| PayPal | 1–3 business days |
| Nova Gift Card | Immediately credited to gift card balance |
| Buy Now Pay Later | Per provider terms (typically 5–10 business days) |

Refunds are issued once the returned item has been inspected and approved at our fulfilment centre. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is processed.

---

## 7. Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different size, colour, or model, please return the original item and place a new order.

---

## 8. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, contact **support@novaretail.com** within **48 hours** of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free return and ship the correct or replacement item at no additional charge.
