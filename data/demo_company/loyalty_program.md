# Nova Retail — Nova Rewards Loyalty Programme

**Last updated:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** Customer Loyalty Team

---

## 1. Overview

Nova Rewards is Nova Retail's free loyalty programme that lets you earn points on every purchase and redeem them for discounts on future orders.

**To join:** Create a free account at **novaretail.com/signup**. Membership is free with no annual fee.

---

## 2. Earning Points

| Activity | Points Earned |
|---|---|
| Every $1 spent on Nova-branded products | 3 points |
| Every $1 spent on third-party products | 1 point |
| Account sign-up bonus | 250 points |
| Birthday bonus (each year) | 500 points |
| Product review (limit: 5 per calendar year) | 50 points per review |
| Referring a new customer (first purchase ≥ $30) | 300 points per referral |
| Nova app download (one-time) | 100 points |

Points are credited to your account within **24 hours** of order delivery confirmation.

Points are earned on the price paid after discounts but **before** tax and shipping.

---

## 3. Redeeming Points

**Conversion rate:** 100 points = $1.00 discount

**Minimum redemption:** 500 points ($5.00)  
**Maximum redemption per order:** 10,000 points ($100.00)

To redeem at checkout:
1. Ensure you are logged in to your Nova Rewards account.
2. At the payment step, select **"Use My Points"**.
3. Enter the number of points you wish to redeem or tap **"Use Maximum."**
4. Points are deducted immediately upon order confirmation.

Points used on a cancelled or returned order are automatically re-credited within **3 business days** of the refund being issued.

---

## 4. Points Expiry

Points expire if your account has **no qualifying purchase activity for 18 consecutive months**. A qualifying purchase is any order with a value of $5.00 or more.

You will receive email reminders at **90, 30, and 7 days** before expiry. Making a qualifying purchase at any time resets the 18-month clock for all existing points.

---

## 5. Nova Gift Cards

Nova Retail offers Gift Cards in denominations of **$25, $50, $100, $200, and $500**, available for purchase at novaretail.com or in-store.

- Gift cards can be used online (enter the code at checkout) or in-store (present the physical card or code).
- Up to **5 gift cards** can be applied to a single order.
- Gift card balances do not earn Rewards points.
- Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash or returned for a refund.
- Replacement cards can be issued for lost or stolen cards if the original purchase receipt is available.

---

## 6. Membership Tiers

Your tier is determined by total spend in the current **calendar year** (1 Jan – 31 Dec):

| Tier | Annual Spend | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | $0–$499 | 1–3 pts / $1, birthday bonus |
| Silver | $500–$1,499 | 5 % off Nova-branded items, priority support |
| Gold | $1,500–$3,999 | 10 % off all items, free expedited shipping |
| Elite | $4,000+ | 15 % off all items, early sale access, dedicated account manager |

Tier status is evaluated monthly. If you reach a new tier mid-year, benefits apply immediately. Tiers reset to Bronze on 1 January each year unless you have already hit the spend threshold.

---

## 7. Combining Rewards

| Combination | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Rewards points + member tier discount | ✓ Yes |
| Rewards points + promo code | ✓ Yes |
| Rewards points + gift card | ✓ Yes |
| Member tier discount + promo code | ✗ No (greater discount applied) |

---

## 8. Programme Terms

- Nova Rewards is available to US residents aged 18 and over.
- Points have no cash value and are non-transferable.
- Nova Retail reserves the right to modify or terminate the programme with 30 days' notice.
- Fraudulent activity (e.g. fake reviews, self-referrals) will result in forfeiture of all points and account closure.
