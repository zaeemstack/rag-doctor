## In-Store Services

All stores offer:
- **Nova Rewards enrolment** and point redemption
- **In-store returns** (no shipping label required; bring item + proof of purchase)
- **Gift card purchase** (physical and digital)
- **Product demonstrations** (select stores — check store page for availability)

Flagship stores additionally offer:
- **Warranty Service Centre** — bring defective Nova-branded products for assessment
- **B2B consultation** desk

---

## Holiday Hours

Stores operate modified hours on public holidays. Check novaretail.com/stores for holiday hours, which are posted at least **2 weeks** in advance.

---

## Accessibility

All Nova Retail stores are ADA-compliant with step-free access, accessible fitting rooms (where applicable), and staff trained to assist customers with disabilities. Contact the individual store by phone if you have specific accessibility needs.
