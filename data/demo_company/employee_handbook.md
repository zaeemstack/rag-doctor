# Nova Retail — Employee Handbook

**Version:** 6.0 — Effective 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** People & Culture Team  
**Applies to:** All Nova Retail employees (United States)

---

## Welcome

Nova Retail was founded in 2011 with a mission to make great products accessible to everyone. We have grown from a single warehouse in Columbus, Ohio to a team of 4,200 employees across 18 US states. This handbook summarises the policies, expectations, and benefits that govern your employment.

*Full policies are maintained in the HR portal at hr.novaretail.com. In any conflict between this handbook and a specific policy document, the specific policy document takes precedence.*

---

## 1. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity and respect
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary business information
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose potential conflicts to your manager and People & Culture
- Not accept gifts valued over **$50** from vendors or customers without prior written approval
- Comply with all applicable laws and Nova Retail's internal policies

Violations of the code of conduct may result in corrective action up to and including termination of employment.

---

## 2. Working Hours & Attendance

**Corporate employees:**  
Standard hours are 09:00–17:30 (your local time), Monday to Friday, with a 30-minute unpaid lunch break. Flexible start times (07:00–10:00) are available with manager agreement.

**Warehouse & Fulfilment employees:**  
Shift schedules are set monthly and posted at least **10 days** in advance. Standard shifts are 8 hours with two 15-minute paid breaks and one 30-minute unpaid meal break.

Attendance issues must be reported to your manager before your shift starts. Repeated unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 3. Leave Entitlements (Summary)

All full-time staff are entitled to **20 days** of paid annual leave per year, accruing at **1.67 days per month**. This summary is provided for quick reference; the definitive rules on accrual caps, carry-over, and request procedures are documented in the Leave Policy (HR portal).

| Leave Type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Annual Leave | 20 days / year |
| Sick Leave | Up to 10 days / year (accrued at 1 hr / 30 hrs worked) |
| Parental Leave (birthing) | 16 weeks at full pay |
| Parental Leave (non-birthing) | 6 weeks at full pay |
| Bereavement (spouse/child) | 5 days |
| Jury Duty | Up to 10 days at full pay |

---

## 4. Compensation & Payroll

- Pay periods are **bi-weekly** (every two weeks); direct deposit lands on Friday.
- Employees can view pay stubs and update banking details via the payroll portal (**payroll.novaretail.com**).
- Merit reviews are conducted annually in **March**, with any approved increases effective from 1 April.
- Overtime for non-exempt employees is paid at **1.5× the regular hourly rate** for hours beyond 40 per week.

---

## 5. Benefits Overview

### 5.1 Health Insurance
Nova Retail offers three medical plans (Bronze, Silver, Gold) through Aetna, plus dental and vision coverage through MetLife. Open enrolment occurs every **November** for coverage beginning 1 January.

### 5.2 Retirement (401k)
Nova Retail matches **100 % of employee contributions up to 4 %** of base salary. Employees are eligible to enrol from their first day of employment. Matching contributions vest on a **3-year cliff** schedule.

### 5.3 Employee Discount
All employees receive a **25 % discount** on Nova-branded products purchased at novaretail.com or Nova Retail stores, up to $5,000 in annual purchases. The discount cannot be combined with promotional codes or stacked with customer loyalty programmes.

### 5.4 Tuition Reimbursement
Nova Retail reimburses up to **$5,250 per calendar year** for job-related degree programmes or professional certifications. Applications must be approved before enrolment. Minimum grade of B (or equivalent) required for reimbursement.

### 5.5 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
Free, confidential counselling (up to 8 sessions per year) and referral services through Lyra Health. Access via **lyra.com/novaretail** or 1-800-NOVA-EAP.

---

## 6. Remote & Hybrid Work

Corporate employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** with manager agreement. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and a formal remote work agreement. Warehouse and fulfilment roles are on-site only.

---

## 7. Performance & Development

- **90-day check-in:** All new employees meet with their manager and a People & Culture partner within the first 90 days.
- **Mid-year review:** Informal progress discussion in July.
- **Annual review:** Formal performance evaluation in March.
- **Learning Portal:** Access to 10,000+ online courses via LinkedIn Learning at no cost.

---

## 8. Disciplinary Process

Nova Retail follows a progressive disciplinary process:

1. Verbal counselling (documented)
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning / Performance Improvement Plan
4. Termination

Serious misconduct (e.g. harassment, theft, safety violations) may result in immediate termination, skipping earlier steps.

---

## 9. Separation

- **Voluntary resignation:** Minimum **2 weeks' notice** expected (4 weeks for management roles).
- **Accrued annual leave payout:** Paid out upon separation per applicable state law.
- **Equipment return:** All company equipment must be returned within **5 business days** of last day.
