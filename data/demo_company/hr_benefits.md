# Nova Retail — Employee Benefits Guide

**Effective:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** People & Culture Team  
**Applies to:** All full-time US employees

---

## 1. Health & Wellbeing

### Medical Insurance
Nova Retail offers three medical plan options through **Aetna**:

| Plan | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max | Network |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze HSA | $45 | $2,000 | $6,500 | Aetna Open Access |
| Silver PPO | $120 | $750 | $4,000 | Aetna Open Choice |
| Gold PPO | $210 | $250 | $2,500 | Aetna Open Choice |

Nova Retail contributes **$150/month** toward the premium for all plans. Family coverage is available; premiums scale by coverage level.

### Dental Insurance
Through **MetLife Dental**. Covers 100 % of preventive care, 80 % of basic procedures (fillings, extractions), and 50 % of major procedures (crowns, bridges) after a $50 annual deductible. Annual maximum benefit: **$2,000 per person**.

### Vision Insurance
Through **MetLife Vision**. Annual exam fully covered (in-network); $200 allowance for frames or contacts per year.

### Health Savings Account (HSA)
Employees enrolled in the Bronze HSA plan receive a **$600/year** contribution from Nova Retail to their HSA. Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit.

## 2. Retirement

### 401(k) Plan
- **Eligibility:** From day one of employment.
- **Employer match:** Nova Retail matches **100 % of employee contributions up to 4 %** of base salary.
- **Vesting:** Employer match vests on a **3-year cliff** (0 % in years 1–2, 100 % in year 3+).
- **Funds:** Access to a curated selection of Vanguard index funds and target-date funds.
- **Roth option:** Both traditional pre-tax and Roth post-tax contributions are available.

---

## 3. Employee Discount

- **25 % off** all Nova-branded products purchased at novaretail.com or Nova Retail stores.
- Applicable to purchases up to **$5,000 per calendar year**.
- Cannot be combined with customer promo codes or customer loyalty programme discounts.
- Eligible from first day of employment.
- Discount extends to one household family member (one designated person per employee per year).

---

## 4. Tuition Reimbursement

Nova Retail reimburses up to **$5,250 per calendar year** for:
- Job-related undergraduate or graduate degree programmes
- Professional certifications (e.g. PMP, CPA, AWS, Salesforce)
- Language courses relevant to the employee's role

**Process:**
1. Submit a pre-approval request via the HR portal before enrolment.
2. Complete the programme with a minimum grade of **B** (or "Pass" for pass/fail courses).
3. Submit receipts via HR portal within **60 days** of course completion.

Reimbursement is clawed back (pro-rated) if the employee leaves Nova Retail within **12 months** of receiving a reimbursement.

## 5. Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

Free and **confidential** support through **Lyra Health**:
- Up to **8 counselling sessions per year** at no cost (individual or couples therapy)
- Mental health coaching, meditation, and self-guided programmes
- Financial counselling: 3 free sessions with a certified financial planner
- Legal advice: 30-minute consultations for personal legal matters (unlimited)

**Access:** lyra.com/novaretail | 1-800-NOVA-EAP | Available 24/7

---

## 6. Parental Support

- **Parental leave:** As detailed in the Leave Policy (16 weeks for birthing parent, 6 weeks for non-birthing parent, both at full pay).
- **Back-to-work support:** A phased return programme (50 % schedule for 4 weeks) available after parental leave.
- **Childcare subsidy:** $1,000 / year in childcare subsidy via Dependent Care FSA employer contribution.
- **Lactation rooms:** Available in all corporate offices and flagship stores.

---

## 7. Commuter Benefits

Employees may contribute up to the IRS monthly pre-tax limit for transit and vanpool expenses via a **Commuter Benefit Account**. Nova Retail contributes an additional **$50/month** toward commuter costs.

Benefits enrolment occurs every **November** (for coverage beginning 1 January). New hires have **30 days** from their start date to enrol in health, dental, and vision plans outside of open enrolment.
