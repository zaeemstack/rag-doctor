# Nova Retail — Human Resources: Leave Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** People & Culture Team  
**Version:** 4.1  
**Applies to:** All full-time employees in the United States

---

## 1. Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)

Full-time employees accrue **15 days** of annual leave per calendar year, earned at a rate of **1.25 days per month**. Leave begins accruing from the first day of employment.

### 1.1 Accrual Cap

Accrued annual leave is capped at **22.5 days** (1.5× the annual entitlement). Once the cap is reached, no further leave accrues until the balance drops below the cap.

### 1.2 Carry-Over

Up to **5 days** of unused annual leave may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December each year.

### 1.3 Requesting Annual Leave

- Submit requests via the HR portal (**hr.novaretail.com**) at least **5 business days** in advance for absences of 1–3 days.
- For absences of 4+ consecutive days, submit at least **10 business days** in advance.
- Requests are subject to manager approval and business needs.

### 1.4 Leave Payout on Termination

Accrued and unused annual leave is paid out at the employee's current base salary rate upon separation from Nova Retail, in accordance with applicable state law.

---

## 2. Sick Leave

All employees accrue **1 hour of sick leave for every 30 hours worked**, up to a maximum of **10 days (80 hours)** per calendar year. Sick leave may be used for:

- Personal illness or injury
- Medical, dental, or mental health appointments
- Caring for an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling)
- Domestic violence leave as required by applicable law

Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out upon termination.

---

## 3. Public Holidays

Nova Retail observes the following **11 paid public holidays** per year:

1. New Year's Day (1 January)
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (3rd Monday in January)
3. Presidents' Day (3rd Monday in February)
4. Memorial Day (last Monday in May)
5. Independence Day (4 July)
6. Labor Day (1st Monday in September)
7. Columbus Day (2nd Monday in October)
8. Veterans Day (11 November)
9. Thanksgiving Day (4th Thursday in November)
10. Day after Thanksgiving
11. Christmas Day (25 December)

When a public holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

---

## 4. Parental Leave

| Event | Duration | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Birth (birthing parent) | 16 weeks | 100 % of base salary |
| Birth (non-birthing parent / partner) | 6 weeks | 100 % of base salary |
| Adoption | 8 weeks | 100 % of base salary |
| Foster placement (≥ 90 days) | 4 weeks | 100 % of base salary |

Parental leave must be taken within **12 months** of the qualifying event. Employees must have completed at least **6 months** of continuous service to be eligible.

---

## 5. Bereavement Leave

| Relationship | Paid Days |
|---|---|
| Spouse / domestic partner / child | 5 days |
| Parent / sibling / grandparent | 3 days |
| Other immediate family | 1 day |

Additional unpaid leave may be granted at manager discretion.

---

## 6. Jury Duty & Civic Leave

Employees summoned for jury duty receive **full pay** for up to **10 business days**. Beyond 10 days, employees receive the difference between jury duty pay and their regular salary. Civic leave (e.g. voting, serving as an election official) is granted with up to **4 hours** of paid time per election event.

---

## 7. Unpaid Leave of Absence

Employees who have exhausted their paid leave entitlements may apply for an unpaid leave of absence of up to **3 months** per rolling 12-month period. Applications must be submitted to People & Culture at least **2 weeks** in advance and are subject to business approval.
