# Nova Retail — Payment Methods

**Last updated:** 15 March 2025  
**Document owner:** Payments Team

---

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods

### 1.1 Credit & Debit Cards
Nova Retail accepts the following card networks:

- **Visa** (credit and debit)
- **Mastercard** (credit and debit)
- **American Express**
- **Discover**

Cards must have a valid billing address. Prepaid cards are accepted but refunds will be issued to the original prepaid card; if the card is no longer active, store credit will be issued instead.

### 1.2 PayPal
Select PayPal at checkout to be redirected to PayPal's secure payment page. Both PayPal balance and PayPal-linked bank accounts/cards are supported.

### 1.3 Apple Pay & Google Pay
Available in compatible browsers (Safari for Apple Pay; Chrome/Edge for Google Pay). Payment is completed with biometric authentication on your device — no card details are entered on the Nova Retail site.

### 1.4 Nova Gift Cards
Gift cards are accepted online and in-store.

- Enter the **16-digit gift card code** in the "Payment" field at checkout.
- Up to **5 gift cards** may be applied per order.
- If the gift card balance does not cover the full order total, you may pay the remainder with another method.
- Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Replacement cards for lost or stolen gift cards require the original purchase receipt.

### 1.5 Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)

**Klarna** — Pay in 4 interest-free instalments over 6 weeks. No hard credit check. Available for orders between $35 and $1,500.

**Afterpay** — Pay in 4 instalments every 2 weeks over 8 weeks. Available for orders up to $2,000. Late fees may apply per Afterpay's terms.

BNPL options appear at checkout when eligible. Approval is subject to the BNPL provider's assessment.

### 1.6 Nova Rewards Points
Rewards points earned in the Nova Rewards programme can be applied at checkout alongside any payment method (100 points = $1.00). Minimum redemption is 500 points.

---

## 2. Currency

All transactions on novaretail.com are processed in **US dollars (USD)**. For international customers, your bank or payment provider will apply their exchange rate and any applicable foreign transaction fees.

---

## 3. Payment Security

- All payment data is transmitted over **TLS 1.3** encrypted connections.
- Nova Retail is **PCI-DSS Level 1** certified; card numbers are never stored on Nova Retail's servers.
- Transactions are processed by our certified payment gateway partner.
- 3D Secure (Verified by Visa / Mastercard SecureCode) is supported and may be triggered by your bank.

---

## 4. Invoice & Receipt

A digital receipt is emailed within 5 minutes of order confirmation. Business customers who need a formal invoice for accounting purposes can request one via **b2b@novaretail.com**.

---

## 5. Failed Payments

If a payment fails:

1. You will see an on-screen error message specifying the reason (where known).
2. No charge is made to your account for a failed transaction.
3. Try a different card or payment method.
4. If the problem persists, contact your bank — they may be blocking the transaction for fraud prevention.

---

## 6. Authorisation Holds

Some payment methods (particularly debit cards) may show an **authorisation hold** for the order amount immediately after checkout. This hold is released when:

- The order ships (at which point the actual charge is finalised), or
- The order is cancelled (hold released within 3–7 business days, depending on your bank).
