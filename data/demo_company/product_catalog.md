## About Nova-Branded Products

Nova Retail's private-label line — "Nova" — spans consumer electronics, home goods, and accessories. Every Nova-branded product is designed in-house and manufactured to our quality specifications.

Every Nova-branded product comes with a **1-year** limited manufacturer's warranty as standard (aligned with Warranty Policy). See the Warranty Policy document for claim procedures and coverage details.
