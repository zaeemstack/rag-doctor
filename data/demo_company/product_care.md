# Nova Retail — Product Care & Maintenance

**Last updated:** 1 January 2025  
**Document owner:** Product Quality Team

---

## General Care Principles

Proper care extends product lifespan and preserves warranty coverage. Damage caused by improper maintenance is not covered under the Nova Retail Limited Warranty.

---

## Nova Electronics

### Nova Hub Pro & Smart Plugs
- Keep away from moisture and direct sunlight.
- Do not block ventilation slots on the Nova Hub Pro.
- Clean exterior surfaces with a **dry microfibre cloth** only. Do not use spray cleaners directly on the device.
- For firmware updates, use the Nova app — updates are applied automatically unless disabled in settings.
- Reset procedure: press and hold the reset button for **10 seconds** until the LED flashes amber.

### Nova Audio (SoundBar, Buds)
**SoundBar X1:**
- Clean the speaker grille with a soft brush or lint-free cloth. Do not insert objects into the grille.
- Avoid placing the soundbar on surfaces that block rear-port bass reflex.
- Recommended operating temperature: **0 °C – 40 °C (32 °F – 104 °F)**.

**Nova Buds Air:**
- Clean ear tips with a slightly damp cloth; allow to dry fully before reinserting.
- Store in the charging case when not in use to preserve battery health.
- Do not expose to temperatures below **−10 °C (14 °F)** or above **45 °C (113 °F)**.
- Battery health tip: avoid leaving the case fully discharged for extended periods.

## Nova Kitchen Appliances

### Nova Precision Blender
- Wash the container, lid, and blade assembly after every use. All components are **dishwasher-safe (top rack)**.
- Self-cleaning cycle: fill the container halfway with warm water and a drop of dish soap, then run the "Clean" preset for 30 seconds. Rinse and air-dry.
- Never submerge the motor base in water. Wipe with a damp cloth only.
- Do not blend hot liquids above **70 °C (158 °F)** without venting the lid.

### Nova Coffee Station
- Descale the machine every **3 months** (or when the descale indicator activates) using Nova-recommended descaling solution or a mixture of 50 % white vinegar and 50 % water.
- Run 2 full water cycles after descaling to purge residue.
- Clean the carafe and brew basket in the dishwasher or with warm soapy water after each use.
- Empty and clean the drip tray weekly.

## Nova Wearables

### Nova FitBand Pro
- The band is rated **5 ATM** — safe for swimming and showering, but not for scuba diving or high-pressure water sports.
- Rinse with fresh water after exposure to salt water, chlorine, or sweat.
- Clean the sensor pod and underside with a dry cloth; avoid soap or chemical cleaners on the skin-facing sensors.
- Charge using only the included magnetic charging cable. Do not use third-party chargers that exceed 5 W.
- Battery care: charge the band when it reaches **10–15 %** to optimise long-term battery lifespan.

---

## Nova Accessories

### Nova USB-C Hub
- Do not connect more than one 4K display simultaneously — only one HDMI output is active at a time.
- The hub generates heat during heavy use; ensure adequate airflow and do not place in enclosed spaces.
- Disconnect from your laptop before storing in a bag to avoid port stress.

## Storage Guidelines

| Product Type | Recommended Storage |
|---|---|
| Electronics (general) | Cool, dry place; 5–35 °C (41–95 °F); relative humidity < 80 % |
| Earbuds | In the charging case; away from extreme temperatures |
| Blender / Coffee Station | Clean and dry; store at room temperature |
| FitBand | Charged to 50–80 % if storing for more than 2 months |

---

## Warranty Implications

Damage resulting from failure to follow these care instructions is considered user damage and is **not covered** by the Nova Retail Limited Warranty or the Nova Protection Plan. Always retain original packaging for warranty service shipments.
